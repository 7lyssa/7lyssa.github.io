Enterprise Network Engineering & Infrastructure Project

Jan 2026 – May 2026

Purdue University

  • Built a multi-lab enterprise network from physical cabling through routing, security, and access control across Cisco and HP/Aruba hardware.
  • Terminated and tested Cat6 twisted-pair cable to TIA-568A/B standards; analyzed Ethernet and FTP traffic with Wireshark and SPAN port mirroring.
  • Deployed VyOS and Cisco ISR 4331 routers with VLAN segmentation (802.1Q), inter-VLAN routing via router-on-a-stick, DHCP, NAT/PAT, and SSH management.
  • Configured a VyOS wireless access point with WPA security and subnet segmentation across wired and wireless clients.
  • Implemented Multiple Spanning Tree Protocol across a mixed-vendor three-switch topology to eliminate Layer 2 loops.
  • Configured OSPF dynamic routing and extended ACLs to enforce department-level access restrictions.
